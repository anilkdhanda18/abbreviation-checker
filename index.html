Proofing report

This is a heuristic aid, not a substitute for a careful read. Detection of acronyms, definitions, and section boundaries is pattern-based and can still miss cases or over-flag chemical formulae and all-caps words. Definitions are recognised when the acronym sits beside its parenthetical (either order), including phrasings like "hereafter" or "abbreviated as". Requirements vary by journal, so always confirm against your target journal's author guidelines.

What this checks
  1. Define at first use. Every non-standard abbreviation should be spelled out in full the first time it appears, with the short form in parentheses.
  2. Do not use before defining. The full form must appear at or before the first use, not later.
  3. Earn the abbreviation. Defined terms that are then used only once or twice are flagged.
  4. No abbreviations in the title unless universally recognised (checked when a title is provided).
  5. Plurals without apostrophes. MFCs, not MFC's.
  6. Do not begin a sentence with an abbreviation.
  7. Define once. Repeated redefinition is flagged; a separate definition in the abstract versus the main text is acceptable.
  8. Units are not pluralised (kg, not kgs).
  9. List of abbreviations. Suggested when many abbreviations are present and no such section is found.
  10. References are skipped by default so citations and journal acronyms do not cause false flags.
  11. Tables are skipped by default. Word tables are removed directly, and numeric data rows with their header line are filtered from PDF and pasted text.
